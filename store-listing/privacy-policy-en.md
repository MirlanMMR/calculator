# Calculator: Themes & History — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Calculator: Themes & History ("the app") is a calculator for Android made by MMR Lab ("the developer", "we"). This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: your data stays on your phone, we have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share it.

## What the app stores

Your settings (theme, key shape, layout, the keys you moved in the constructor), the numbers on the calculator screens, and the history of results are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive them.

## What we collect

Nothing. The developer does not run any server for the app, has no account system, and receives no data from the app. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and no third-party trackers.

## Permissions and internet

The app requests no permissions and has no internet access: it cannot send anything off your phone.

## Clipboard

The app puts a number or a calculation on the clipboard only when you ask it to copy. It reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and uses it only to take a number from it. Nothing from the clipboard is stored or sent anywhere.

## Android's own backup

Your Android device may include the app's data in its regular backup to your Google account if you have that backup turned on in the phone settings. This is done by Android under Google's terms; the developer has no access to it.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

## Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your phone until you clear the history (the clear button on the History screen), clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing the app's data deletes it from your device.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
