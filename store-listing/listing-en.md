## Full description (max 4000)

A calculator you arrange yourself, with 16 themes, a full history and scientific functions. Choose where the zero goes, where the operators sit, how the keys look, and move any key anywhere. No ads, no accounts, no internet needed.

YOUR LAYOUT
• Six layouts for the zero key: top or bottom, left, centre or right. The wide zero sits where your thumb lands.
• Operators on the right or on the left, in an order that always keeps minus above plus.
• AC, %, backspace and equals always stay on the top row.

THE CONSTRUCTOR
• Tap the pencil and drag any key to a new place; the neighbours slide away to make room.
• Tap a key to take it off, tap an empty cell to put any key back, even new ones from the function strip.
• Hold the zero to make it wide or narrow.
• Your layout is saved automatically.

LOOKS
• 16 colour themes, 8 dark and 8 light.
• 5 key shapes: hexagon, circle, soft square, sharp square and square.

SCIENTIFIC WHEN YOU NEED IT
• Tap fx to open a strip of functions: square root, square, power, 1/x, sin, cos, tan, ln, log, pi and e.
• Trigonometry works in degrees.
• Pin the ones you use most onto the keypad.

WORK ON SEVERAL THINGS AT ONCE
• Swipe the display sideways to get a fresh screen; come back and your previous calculation is still there. Three screens are always ready, up to five.
• A blinking cursor: tap any digit to fix a mistake without retyping the whole number.
• Long-press the display to copy the number or paste one from another app.

HISTORY
• Every result is saved in a full-screen history. Tap an entry to bring its result back to the calculator, hold it to copy the whole calculation.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline. The app asks for no permissions and has no internet access.
• No ads, no tracking, no account. Everything stays on your phone.

The interface is in English and uses simple icons.
